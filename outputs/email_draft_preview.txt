### 5. Standardized Explanation Snippet: Depository Participant (DP) Charge

* **Definition:** A Depository Participant (DP) charge is a flat regulatory fee levied per company (ISIN), per trading day, whenever equity holdings are sold from your Demat account.
* **Beneficiaries:** This fee is collected by the depositories (CDSL/NSDL) and the depository participant; it is not an intraday platform brokerage charge.
* **Application Rule:** The charge is independent of the number of shares sold (e.g., selling 1 share or 100 shares of the same company on the same day incurs the exact same flat fee).
* **Billing Method:** The fee is automatically debited from your trading balance/ledger at the end of the settlement cycle, not deducted directly from your linked bank account.
