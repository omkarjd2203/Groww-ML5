# Groww AI Review Intelligence & Action Pipeline (MCP-Gated)

## Project Overview
This project builds an automated AI product workflow for the Groww platform. It ingests raw, unstructured customer reviews, clusters them into actionable themes using LLMs (Gemini API), identifies recurring customer confusion regarding fees, generates structured product and support deliverables, and executes downstream tool calls via Model Context Protocol (MCP) with human-in-the-loop approval.

---

## What Fee Issue Was Identified From Reviews
* **Identified Fee Issue**: **Depository Participant (DP) Charge** (₹13.50 + 18% GST = ₹15.93 per stock company per day).
* **Root Cause**: Customers frequently misinterpret flat depository delivery sales charges (levied by CDSL and Groww) as "hidden or unexpected fees" because the deduction is reflected post-trade in contract notes rather than on the pre-trade order confirmation screen.
* **Operational Impact**: Leads to 1-star reviews, customer distrust upon selling delivery shares, and a significant spike in CSAT billing support tickets.
